# Problem Statement

## 1. Difficulty Selecting Appropriate Exercises

Beginners often struggle to choose exercises that match their fitness level and experience. Some exercises require balance, coordination, or specific techniques that may be difficult for inexperienced users. Without proper guidance, users may select unsuitable exercises that reduce effectiveness or increase injury risk.

## 2. Difficulty Accessing Personalized Exercise Guidance

Many beginners do not have access to professional trainers due to budget limitations or location constraints. As a result, they often rely on generic workout plans that may not match their fitness goals, available equipment, or experience level.

## 3. Difficulty Maintaining and Monitoring Progress

Users frequently fail to track their workout activities consistently. Without a proper record of completed workouts and performance data, it becomes difficult to monitor improvement, maintain motivation, and stay committed to fitness goals.

## Proposed Solution

The AI Fitness Coaching application addresses these challenges by using CNN-based image classification and personalized workout recommendations. The system helps users select suitable exercises, receive customized guidance, and monitor their workout history and progress.
